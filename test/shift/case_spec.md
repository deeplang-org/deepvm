## shift/rotate testcase spec

覆盖 wasm 1.0 的整数移位与循环移位指令（10 条 opcode，每条 3 个用例）。
移位量按位宽取模（`i32` 取模 32，`i64` 取模 64）。`.wasm` 由脚本手工生成；
main 返回结果，i32 以无符号、i64 以有符号打印。

- `i32_shl_001/002/003`：`1<<4`=16；`1<<32`（32%32=0）=1；`0xF<<8`=3840
- `i32_shrs_001/002/003`（算术右移）：`-8>>1`=-4；`-1>>31`=-1；`1>>0`=1
- `i32_shru_001/002/003`（逻辑右移）：`-8>>1`=0x7FFFFFFC；`1>>0`=1；`8>>1`=4
- `i64_shl_001/002/003`：`1<<40`=1099511627776；`1<<63`=0x8000000000000000；`0xF<<8`=3840
- `i64_shrs_001/002/003`（算术）：`-8>>1`=-4；`-1>>63`=-1；`1>>0`=1
- `i64_shru_001/002/003`（逻辑）：`-1>>1`=0x7FFFFFFFFFFFFFFF；`1>>0`=1；`8>>1`=4
- `i32_rotl_001/002/003`：`0x80000001 rotl 1`=3；`0x34567890 rotl 5`=878082066；`1 rotl 31`=0x80000000
- `i32_rotr_001/002/003`：`1 rotr 1`=0x80000000；`0x80000000 rotr 1`=0x40000000；`0x34567890 rotr 11`=2014458966
- `i64_rotl_001/002/003`：`0x8000000000000001 rotl 1`=3；`1 rotl 63`=0x8000000000000000；`0x56789ABCDEF01234 rotl 5`=6230889152035885620
- `i64_rotr_001/002/003`：`1 rotr 1`=0x8000000000000000；`0x8000000000000000 rotr 1`=0x4000000000000000；`0x56789ABCDEF01234 rotr 11`=-3607644760502136661
